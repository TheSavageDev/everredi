# EverRedi product (v1)

## What it is

EverRedi helps people track emergency-prep and first-aid kits: supplies, quantities, expirations, and kit completeness — alone or with collaborators.

## MVP in scope

- Auth: email, Google, Apple (Supabase)
- Workspaces with many members (`owner | admin | member`)
- Locations, supply catalog, freeform items, kits, inventory
- Kit templates (seeded starter set)
- Expiration and low-stock alerts
- Share links (view/edit)
- RevenueCat Pro entitlement: `everredi-pro`
- Free limits: 5 kits, 100 inventory items, 2 locations, capped members
- Pricing reference: $4.99/mo, $39.99/yr, $99.99 lifetime
- Web marketing + app, Expo iOS/Android
